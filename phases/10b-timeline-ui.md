# 10b Timeline UI

Read: plan/02-core-loop.md (screens), plan/03-game-modes.md (Timeline).

## Do
Phone: own timeline as a scrollable ordered row of year cards, tappable insertion slots (large targets, clear highlight, confirm and change until time is up), artist text box, result feedback after reveal. Host: all players' progress (card counts), reveal view, who placed correctly, who hit the artist. Mobile-first.

## Gate
- App builds; component tests for slot logic including equal years; scripted end-to-end run with 3 simulated players through one full round and a win. Final message counts only.
